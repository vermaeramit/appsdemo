# Blog publishing schedule

Living doc — update statuses as posts go out. Cadence is **1 post every 2 weeks**
(2 posts per month). Slipping is fine; gaps over 6 weeks hurt SEO momentum.

## Cadence policy

- **Target:** publish 1 post every 2 weeks (alternating Wednesdays)
- **Minimum:** at least 1 post per calendar month — never skip
- **Quality bar:** ~1500–2500 words, citing real numbers (no fabricated stats), with one soft CTA into a relevant `/services/*` or `/products/*` page
- **Review before publish:** read it once aloud — if it sounds AI-generic, rewrite the opening

## What to do 5 days before each publish date

1. Tell me which post we're shipping. I draft.
2. You review, mark up real anecdotes / numbers / client quotes.
3. Final tweak + ship — usually 1 hour of your time per post once you're warmed up.
4. After publish: GSC → URL Inspection → **Request indexing** on the new URL.
5. Optional: post on LinkedIn with 2-3 sentence teaser linking to the article. Cheapest distribution you've got.
